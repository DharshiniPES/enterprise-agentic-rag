# Tesla, Inc. Q2 FY2024 Financial Update & AI Deployment Metrics
**Filing Date:** July 23, 2024  
**Reporting Period:** Quarter Ended June 30, 2024 (Unaudited)

---

## 1. Automotive Operations & Production Summary

Tesla produced **410,831** vehicles and delivered **443,956** vehicles during Q2 2024. Automotive revenues were impacted by price reductions across Model 3 and Model Y, partially offset by a surge in regulatory credits.

### Deliveries and Production Breakdown
| Model Line | Q2 2024 Production | Q2 2024 Deliveries | Q2 2023 Deliveries | YoY Variance (%) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Model 3 / Model Y** | 386,576 | 422,405 | 446,915 | -5.48% |
| **Other Models (S, X, Cybertruck)** | 24,255 | 21,551 | 19,225 | +12.10% |
| **Total Fleet** | **410,831** | **443,956** | **466,140** | **-4.76%** |

*Regulatory Credit Impact:* Automotive regulatory credits recognized reached an all-time high of **$890 million** in Q2 2024, compared to $282 million in Q2 2023, representing a **215.6% increase** due to non-compliance penalties faced by legacy automotive OEMs.

## 2. Energy Storage & Megapack Deployments

Energy Storage revenue grew **99.7% YoY** to **$3.014 billion**, driven by record utility-scale Megapack deployments.

| Operational Metric | Q2 2024 | Q2 2023 | YoY Growth |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Energy Storage Deployed (GWh) | **9.40 GWh** | 3.65 GWh | **+157.5%** |
| Energy Generation & Storage Revenue | $3,014M | $1,509M | +99.7% |
| Energy Segment Gross Margin | 24.55% | 18.42% | +613 bps |
| Lathrop Megafactory Annualized Run-Rate | 40 GWh / year | 10 GWh / year | +300% |

*Operational Commentary:* The Megapack production ramp at the Lathrop, California Megafactory achieved an annualized production run rate of 40 GWh. Construction continues on the Shanghai Megafactory, expected to begin volume production in Q1 2025.

## 3. Artificial Intelligence, FSD V12, and Compute Cluster Expansion

Tesla significantly accelerated its AI compute infrastructure, scaling dedicated training clusters for end-to-end neural network autonomous driving (FSD V12.5).

1. **Training Compute Capacity:** Tesla brought online an additional 15,000 Nvidia H100 GPUs at the Texas Gigafactory (Cortex cluster), reaching a total fleet compute equivalent of **35,000 H100 GPU equivalents**.
2. **Cumulative FSD Miles Driven:** Surpassed **1.6 billion cumulative miles** driven on Full Self-Driving (Supervised), with FSD V12 (end-to-end neural networks) accounting for over 50% of recent miles.
3. **Cybercab & Robotaxi Architecture:** Development continued on the Purpose-Built Robotaxi vehicle, utilizing the unboxed manufacturing process designed to reduce sub-assembly footprint by 35%.
4. **Optimus Gen-2 Progress:** Two autonomous Optimus humanoid robots were deployed in production handling battery cell sorting tasks at the Fremont factory.
