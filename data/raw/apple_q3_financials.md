# Apple Inc. Q3 FY2024 Financial Statement & Operational Review
**Filing Date:** August 1, 2024  
**Reporting Period:** Three Months Ended June 29, 2024 (Unaudited)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Consolidated Results

Apple Inc. reported quarterly revenue of **$85.78 billion**, an increase of **4.87%** year-over-year compared to $81.80 billion in Q3 FY2023. Net income for the quarter was **$21.45 billion**, yielding diluted earnings per share (EPS) of **$1.40**, up 11.1% compared to $1.26 in the prior year quarter.

## 4. Liquidity, Capital Returns, and Apple Intelligence Capital Outlay

1. **Cash & Marketable Securities:** Apple ended the quarter with **$153.0 billion** in total cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities. Total debt stood at $101.3 billion, resulting in net cash of $51.7 billion.
2. **Operating Cash Flow:** Generated **$28.9 billion** in operating cash flow during the quarter.
3. **Shareholder Returns:** Returned **$32.2 billion** to shareholders, consisting of $3.9 billion in cash dividends ($0.25 per share) and $25.0 billion in open market share repurchases (retiring 139 million shares).
4. **Apple Intelligence Infrastructure Investment:** Capital expenditures (CapEx) totaled $2.15 billion, with approximately 35% designated for Private Cloud Compute server nodes powered by custom Apple Silicon (M2 Ultra clusters) supporting privacy-first generative AI features.
